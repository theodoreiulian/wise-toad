The Wise Toad

A meditating toad on a lily pad at the edge of a meadow. Sit with him a while and ask him anything.

The Wise Toad has sat by this pond longer than anyone remembers. He has no robe and no rules, only a long habit of noticing things, and he will tell you what he has noticed if you ask.

Two centuries ago the Zen monk Sengai painted a frog beside a small joke: if sitting in meditation made you a Buddha, the frog would have got there first.

坐禅して人が仏になるならば

The toad agrees. Sitting is only where it starts. You still have to look, and listen.

Say hello and he says hello back. Bring him something heavy, a loss, a fear, a choice you can't make, and he slows down. He draws on the Buddha and Lao Tzu, Zhuangzi, Marcus Aurelius and Camus, and he loves the question a little more than the answer.
