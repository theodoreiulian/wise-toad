## Design Context

### Users
Visitors come to a peaceful pixel-art meadow to talk with a wise toad. The scene follows their local time and weather.

### Brand Personality
Calm, whimsical, contemplative. The user wants loading copy and small animations centered on waking the toad.

### Aesthetic Direction
Keep the existing VT323 type, crisp pixel shapes, moss greens, muted night sky, and warm golden accents. Motion should feel like breathing and quiet life in the meadow.

### Design Principles
- Keep the toad at the center of the experience.
- Match the existing pixel-art scene rather than introducing a new visual style.
- Use small, lightweight animations and honor reduced-motion preferences.
- Never prolong loading to show an animation.
